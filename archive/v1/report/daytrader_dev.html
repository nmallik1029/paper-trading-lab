How it works

  • Data: 5-minute bars from Alpaca's full-market feed since 2016, official trading hours only (early-close days end at 13:00).
  • Decisions at 10:00, 10:30, …, 15:30. Each uses only bars finished by then and fills at the next bar's open; the last block ends at the closing price. Nothing is held overnight.
  • Features: recent returns (5, 15, 30 minutes, since the open, the overnight gap, yesterday, the last 5 days), recent volatility, volume versus normal for that time of day, the time of day, and which ETF.
  • Target: the return over the next 30 minutes (capped at ±1.5% during training).
  • Rule: buy the ETF with the higher prediction when it beats the round-trip cost (2 bps); keep holding while the prediction stays positive; otherwise cash.
  • Walk-forward: retrained at the start of each month on all earlier months (at least 12), so it never sees the month it trades.
  • Cash earns the T-bill rate: the account is always in cash at the close, when brokers calculate interest.
  • Not modeled: delays in getting real-time data, partial fills, and taxes (every gain is short-term). Past results are not a forecast.
