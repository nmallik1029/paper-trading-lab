Method and caveats

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance dividend-adjusted closes, so returns include dividends and are after ETF fees.
  • Universe: SPY plus the nine original Select Sector SPDR ETFs. All existed for the whole period, which avoids survivorship bias (testing only on companies that survived to today).
  • No look-ahead: each signal uses closes up to the month-end and trades at the next trading day's close.
  • Costs: 5 bps per dollar traded for spread and slippage; commissions assumed $0.
  • Parameters were fixed at standard values before running (12-month momentum skipping the latest month, top 3 sectors, 200-day average), not tuned on this data.
  • Not modeled: taxes (the trading strategies realize gains every year; buy-and-hold defers them), fractional-share rounding, and intraday execution.
  • t-stats assume independent daily returns. Treat them as rough.
  • Past results are not a forecast.
