How it works

  • On its first morning the model trains on the 5 trading days before it (about 120 examples: 12 half-hours × 2 ETFs × 5 days).
  • Every morning after that it retrains on everything since, about 24 more examples a day, and never forgets anything.
  • Same features, trading rule, costs and timing as the full-history day trader; see the day trader report.
  • The fresh starts overlap in time, so they show the range of experiences, not independent results. Past results are not a forecast.
