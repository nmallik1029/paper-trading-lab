Lab v2: the research

What I read, what I tested, and why these three strategies are live. Development periods were used to pick; each holdout (daily strategies 2018–today, intraday 2023–today) was looked at once, after the picks were made. 21 variants have been tried in this lab, so passing the strict scorecard now needs t ≥ 3.03. Nothing does; that is normal.

The live lineup

  • Breakout day trader (day-trader account): QQQ's "noise area" is the range it usually moves from the open by each time of day. A break out of it at a half-hour mark means real buying or selling pressure: buy TQQQ (3x long) on an upside break, SQQQ (3x short) on a downside break. Trail a stop at the band or VWAP, sell everything by the close.
  • Multi-strategy fund (moderate account): five unrelated strategies, 20% of the money each, rebalanced daily at 3:40 PM: Bitcoin trend, short volatility, overnight TQQQ, RSI(2) dip buying, and volatility-targeted 3x trend.
  • Learning allocator (safe account): the same five strategies, but the Hedge online-learning algorithm decides each one's share, starting from equal trust on Oct 13, 2026 and moving money toward whatever has been working (with a 5% floor each and slow forgetting).

  • Three strategies for trading volatility ETPs (VIX term structure)
  • Trend-following and mean-reversion in Bitcoin (Quantpedia)
  • The pre-FOMC announcement drift: more recent evidence (New York Fed)
  • The turn-of-the-month effect (CXO Advisory)
  • Connors RSI(2) on the S&P 500, backtest notes

Common thread in the research: most published edges shrink after publication and after realistic costs, so everything here was re-tested on our own data with costs, and the holdouts are the honest guide to expectations.

Risks to know

  • Short volatility can lose most of its value in days (February 2018: -34% for this sleeve in a year; the fund it belongs to was -23% that year).
  • Crypto trend had a -60% drawdown in the holdout.
  • The day trader holds 3x funds; a bad day can cost 5–10%.
  • All backtests ignore taxes. Fake money only.
